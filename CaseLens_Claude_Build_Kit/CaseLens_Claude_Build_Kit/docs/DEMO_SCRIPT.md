# 90–120 Second Golden Demo

## 0–15 sec — Problem

Open CaseLens and say: legal research becomes risky when a citation, paragraph, or proposition is copied without checking its source.

## 15–35 sec — Investigate

Search a landmark case. Open dossier. Show:
- court/date/citation;
- timeline;
- key issue;
- connected authorities.

## 35–55 sec — Graph

Open relationship graph. Drag the current case. Expand one cited case and one statutory provision. Open a prior/later proceeding.

## 55–80 sec — Verify

Upload the curated demo PDF. Show extraction stages. CaseLens identifies multiple citations and flags one known mismatch/unresolved citation.

## 80–100 sec — Evidence

Click the flagged citation. Show claimed metadata beside authoritative match and original source paragraph.

## 100–120 sec — Outcome

Pin the evidence to an Investigation Board and export the Legal Integrity Report.

Closing line:

**CaseLens does not ask users to trust AI. It lets them trace every conclusion back to evidence.**
