# CaseLens Product Specification

## Product statement

**CaseLens — See the full story behind every case.**

CaseLens is an investigative legal intelligence platform focused initially on Indian case law. It combines structured case discovery, procedural chronology, citation networks, evidence inspection, document verification, and investigation reporting.

## Primary users

- advocates and legal researchers;
- law students and academics;
- in-house legal teams;
- judicial/research support teams;
- compliance and insolvency professionals.

## Jobs to be done

### Investigate a case

The user searches by party, citation, court, statute, section, topic, judge, or natural-language issue. Opening a case reveals its full dossier and navigable relationships.

### Trace legal history

The user follows prior proceedings, appeals, later proceedings, and connected matters as a chronological chain.

### Trace precedent

The user can see cited authorities, citing authorities, statutes, sections, and relationship type.

### Verify a document

The user uploads a petition, judgment, written submission, or case brief. CaseLens extracts and checks legal citations, quotations, statutes, and propositions.

### Build an investigation

The user pins cases, paragraphs, statutes, parties, judges, notes, and evidence into a spatial board.

### Export evidence-backed work

CaseLens produces an investigation report or legal integrity report with source provenance.

## Product modes

1. **Explore** — discover cases and topics.
2. **Investigate** — understand one case deeply.
3. **Graph** — trace legal relationships.
4. **Verify** — test citations/claims in a document.
5. **Board** — assemble an investigation.
6. **Report** — export findings.

## MVP success criteria

- Search works over seeded/indexed cases.
- Every case has a dossier page.
- At least one complete multi-stage case timeline exists.
- Graph supports case-to-case and case-to-statute edges.
- User can pin nodes/evidence to an investigation.
- PDF upload extracts text and citations.
- Verification distinguishes metadata match, mismatch, paragraph match, and unresolved.
- Evidence is always inspectable.
- One polished report is exportable.
