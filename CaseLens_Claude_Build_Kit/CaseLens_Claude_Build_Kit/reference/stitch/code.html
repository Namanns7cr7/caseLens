format_quote Operative Ratio Decidendi (Binding Holding) SCR Vol. IV / Page 892

¶ 18. "The protection afforded under Section 14 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 is corporocentric and designed solely to preserve the asset pool of the Corporate Debtor during the corporate insolvency resolution process. It does not, by any canon of statutory interpretation or equity, operate as a personal immunity umbrella for independent personal guarantors whose liability under Section 128 of the Indian Contract Act remains strictly co-extensive, simultaneous, and enforceable without awaiting the outcome of CIRP."

(2021) 9 SCC 321  |  2021 INSC 319 • Coram: L. Nageswara Rao J., S. Ravindra Bhat J. • Decided: 21 May 2021

¶ 125. "Sanction of a resolution plan in respect of a corporate debtor does not operate to discharge the personal guarantors of their liability towards the creditor, given the independent contract under Section 128 of the Indian Contract Act... The release or discharge of a principal debtor by operation of law does not absolve the surety."

(2018) 17 SCC 394  |  2018 INSC 712 • Coram: R.F. Nariman J., Indu Malhotra J. • Decided: 10 August 2018

¶ 26. "Section 14 refers to the corporate debtor only; personal guarantor assets are expressly excluded from the sweep of moratorium. The legislative intention was not to grant a stay on actions against personal guarantors, who continue to be bound by the guarantee contracts under Section 128 of the Contract Act."
